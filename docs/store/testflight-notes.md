# TestFlight: "What to Test"

Paste into the TestFlight group's **What to Test** box (App Store Connect → TestFlight → the build → Test Details). Testers see it in the TestFlight app. Fill in the ALL CAPS part.

```
Thanks for testing Hyber CRM, the app for tutors at centers that use Hyber CRM.

BEFORE YOU START
- Sign in with the email your center has for you (Google, or email and password). If you only see "No access yet", your center hasn't added that address yet: ask them, or try the demo tutor: tutor@demo.hybercrm.com, password DEMO PASSWORD (in HyberCRM_Demo_Accounts.md).
- Allow notifications when the app asks.

PLEASE TRY
1. Today: are today's sessions right, in your center's time?
2. Schedule: open a session. Write the session log for one that has started, and submit it. Did it save while you typed?
3. Availability: set your hours on a day two weeks or more ahead. Try a day in the coming week: the app should say it's locked.
4. News: open an announcement and leave a comment. Did the News badge go down?
5. Notifications: ask your center to change one of your sessions starting within a day (or post an announcement). Did your phone show it, with the app closed? Tapping it should open the session or the announcement.
6. Profile: your account, subjects, payroll history and notification switches.
7. Anything that looked wrong, was slow, or confused you.

HOW TO REPORT
In the TestFlight app: Hyber CRM → Send Beta Feedback (you can attach a screenshot), or write to CONTACT EMAIL. Say what you did, what happened, and the time.
```
